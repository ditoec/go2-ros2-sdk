## Opening hours / Jam buka

The museum is open Tuesday to Sunday, 9 AM to 5 PM. It is closed on Mondays and national holidays.

Museum buka hari Selasa sampai Minggu, pukul 09.00 hingga 17.00. Tutup pada hari Senin dan hari libur nasional.

The last entry is at 4:30 PM, thirty minutes before closing.

## Tickets / Tiket

General admission is 50,000 rupiah for adults and 25,000 rupiah for children under twelve. Children under three enter free.

Tiket masuk dewasa 50.000 rupiah, anak-anak di bawah dua belas tahun 25.000 rupiah. Anak di bawah tiga tahun gratis.

Tickets can be bought at the front desk or online; members enter for free.

## Facilities / Fasilitas

Toilets are located on the ground floor next to the main staircase and on the second floor near the cafe.

Toilet berada di lantai dasar di sebelah tangga utama, dan di lantai dua dekat kafe.

The cafe on the second floor serves coffee, tea, and light snacks until 4:30 PM.

A free cloakroom for bags and coats is available at the entrance.

Wheelchair access is available via the lift near the main entrance; wheelchairs can be borrowed for free at the front desk.

## Exhibits / Pameran

The ground floor hosts the permanent collection of classical paintings from the seventeenth to nineteenth centuries.

The east wing on the first floor holds the dinosaur gallery, featuring a full Tyrannosaurus skeleton cast.

Lantai satu sayap timur memiliki galeri dinosaurus dengan replika kerangka Tyrannosaurus lengkap.

The west wing hosts rotating temporary exhibitions; the current exhibition is about ancient maritime trade routes.

The interactive science lab for children is on the second floor and is open during weekends only.

## Guided tours / Tur berpemandu

Free guided tours in English start at 10 AM and 2 PM daily from the main lobby.

Tur berpemandu gratis dalam Bahasa Indonesia dimulai pukul 11.00 dan 15.00 setiap hari dari lobi utama.

Each tour lasts about forty-five minutes and does not require a reservation.

## Rules / Peraturan

Photography without flash is allowed in all galleries. Flash photography and tripods are not permitted.

Foto tanpa lampu kilat diperbolehkan di semua galeri. Foto dengan lampu kilat dan tripod tidak diperbolehkan.

Food and drinks are only allowed in the cafe area, not inside the galleries.
